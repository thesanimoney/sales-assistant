Technical Context
Post-deployment evaluation of a clinical eligibility classification pipeline. Fine-tuned Llama 3.1 70B performs extraction, then a rule engine applies inclusion–exclusion logic. After a criterion-set change and model swap, clinical reviewers found high false-positive rate dominated by temporal confusion, negation failures, and critical hallucinated findings (evidence absent in EHR).
Objective
Reduce trust and safety risk by lowering hallucination-driven false positives quickly (within next sprint), then structurally improve grounding via verification pass and evaluate retrieval-augmented approach vs re-fine-tuning with span annotations.
